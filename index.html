2140 PD · Rogue AI patrol

You are a police officer. Most robots in the city are good helpers. A few have gone rogue. Find them and detain them.

  1. Look for robots acting strangely: red-flickering eyes, jerky steps, sparks, running away from you.
  2. Scan a robot to check it: point at it and hold Scan.
  3. Detain a rogue robot up close. Detaining a good robot costs reputation.
  4. Get out of the car to walk: fly low and slow first.
